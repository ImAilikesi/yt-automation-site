Creator Publisher

Creator Publisher is a private tool used by one creator to upload videos to the creator's own YouTube channels: Off The Mic, Curious Engine and Timeless Minds. It is not offered to other users.

It uses the YouTube Data API to upload finished videos and set their title, description, captions, thumbnail and scheduled publish time, and to read the name of the channel that was authorised.
